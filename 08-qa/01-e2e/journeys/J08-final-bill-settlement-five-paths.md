**Intent.** BR-BIL-013 says exactly ONE settlement path per final bill — this journey proves all five paths exist and are mutually exclusive by running five move-outs in one week: refund · collect · master-transfer · payment plan · small-balance write-off. Every account carries the same WE-7 deposit position (deposit $150.00 held 14 months @ 2% simple → interest **$3.50** → credit **$153.50** applies FIRST); the frozen WE-7/LE-1 figures anchor paths 1–3.

> ## ⚠ AMENDED 2026-08-24 — CR-030 W5 task 5.13 · FIVE PO RULINGS LAND ON THIS JOURNEY
>
> **The five paths are unchanged and the frozen figures are untouched.** WE-7's
> `$150.00 + $3.50 = $153.50`, LE-1's `289.40 = 264.41 + 24.99` and every figure
> in steps 1–7 below are ❄️ B5 and are not edited here — **if this journey and a
> golden test disagree, the journey is wrong.**
>
> What changed is **what happens BEFORE a path is chosen, and what happens AFTER
> one leaves a balance behind.** Four rulings of 2026-08-24 reach into this
> journey and one of them changes the arithmetic of all five paths:
>
> | Ruling | Where it bites | Steps added |
> |---|---|---|
> | **BR-MET-107** — an unbanked export credit is applied to the final bill | **UPSTREAM OF EVERY PATH.** The credit comes off the bill *before* the deposit settles anything, so a solar account can land on a different path than the same account without one | **3a** |
> | **BR-CUS-096** — an open dispute HOLDS the excess | Step 1's refund. Where any part of what is outstanding is disputed, the excess is **held, not refunded** — a REFUSAL to take path 1 yet, **not a sixth path** | **1a** |
> | **BR-CUS-082** — a debt surviving closure is still chased, ladder restarts at TIER 1 | Step 2's *"normal dunning applies"*, which was true and is no longer precise enough | **2a** |
> | **BR-CUS-097** — a sub-account's unpayable residue moves to its MASTER | Step 3's master-transfer, which J08 reaches by the landlord-agreement flag and BR-CUS-097 reaches by a different trigger entirely | **2a** |
>
> ⚠ **`BR-BIL-013`'s "exactly ONE settlement path" SURVIVES ALL FOUR, and step 7
> still asserts it.** A held excess is not a path, a restarted ladder is not a
> path, and a residue transfer is step 3's path arrived at by another road. **If
> any added step below can be read as a sixth member of the enum, that reading is
> wrong and the step is written badly** — say so rather than widening the enum.
>
> ⚠ **STEP 2a ASSERTS AN AMBIGUITY, ON PURPOSE, AND IT IS THE ONLY STEP HERE THAT
> DOES.** BR-CUS-097 and BR-CUS-082 were ruled the same day and **do not resolve
> each other**: if the residue moves to the master, is the tier-1 ladder run
> against the departed sub-account holder or against the master? Both rules say
> W7 owns the ladder and **must not build both readings**. So 2a pins the
> question rather than a behaviour — a journey that quietly picked one would
> make W7's choice for it, in a file nobody would think to re-read.
