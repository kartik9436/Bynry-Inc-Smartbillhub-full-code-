**Intent.** The largest single build in the product, end to end: an approved
request opens a nine-step wizard that writes **nothing** until its last press;
one commit produces a premises, 26 units, 50 meters, 26 plan assignments and the
**owner's own account**; a crew's work comes back from the field; **a person
accepts it, and that press — not a schedule, not a poller — activates the
service and tells the customer.** The build then sits, correctly, with every
unit vacant and no tenant account anywhere.

**Fixture.** Tenant **brazos-bend** · clerk **D. Ortiz** · owner **Ridge
Property LLC**, *Builder or developer · Construction temporary* · premises
**500 Main Street**, multi-unit residential, parcel `PL-2026-0884`, route
`RT-001 · North AMI` · units **Apt 101–125 + one common area = 26** · services
available at the street **5 of 10** (electric · water · sewer · trash · storm
water) · apartments take **E + W + S**, the common area takes **T + St** ·
plans **Residential Standard** (apartments) and **Commercial Standard** (common
area) · target activation **2026-09-30** · connection **`CON-26-0117`** ·
from request **`SR-26-0043`**.

⚠ **THE QUOTE IS THE REQUEST'S AND IS NOT RE-PRICED.** `$185.00 × 25 = $4,625.00`
· `$310.00 × 24 = $7,440.00` · `$45.00 × 50 = $2,250.00` · `$120.00 × 1 =
$120.00` → **`$14,435.00`**, and it foots. The quantities are internally
consistent: **50 meter sets = 25 electric + 25 water**, and **24 water taps, not
25**, because the common-area unit takes trash and storm water and no water tap.
