**Intent.** The whole ladder and the whole way back, in one account: a debt
climbs tier 1 → 2 → 3, a protection freezes it, the protection lapses, a cut is
SCHEDULED (never cut by the wizard), the cut is committed on the date, the
customer pays, a visit restores service and the row leaves the tab. And, running
underneath all of it, the property §5.8.3 promises the customer: **the ladder
stops itself.** *"If a customer pays or agrees a payment plan, the ladder stops
automatically and the row leaves this list. Nothing here has to be cancelled by
hand."*

**Fixture.** Tenant **brazos-bend** · clerk **Billing Clerk** · account
**`A-11340` · Northwind Mfg. · 77 Industrial Way** · owed **$8,240.00**, oldest
**92 days** · deposit held **$500.00** · services **E W S** · last payment
**$400.00 on 12 June** · notices served **Aug 04 (tier 1) · Aug 18 (tier 2) ·
Aug 22 (tier 3, certified)** · earliest lawful cut **Aug 24** · fees from the
tenant's own charge codes: `CC-DISC` **$45.00**, `CC-RECON` **$75.00**,
`CC-FIELD` **$35.00**.

> ✅ **EVERY FIGURE FOOTS AND IS THE DESIGN'S OWN.** `8,240.00 − 500.00 =
> 7,740.00` is `DSC-02.4`'s deposit arithmetic; **$75.00** is the same number in
> the fee table and in the tier-3 letter — *"the quote and the notice agree"*;
> Aug 04 → Aug 18 is **14 days** against a *10-day* notice, so the ladder waited
> longer than its own minimum, which is the correct direction to err.
