**Intent.** x = occupied unit carrying a WRONG plan (human error at setup); y = corrected plan applied directly on the UNIT side. The boundary matters because there are two doors and they behave differently: the account Change-plan flow (T4-01) prorates mid-cycle; the unit-side edit is the PO-ruled ERROR-CORRECTION override (BR-MET-042, BR-CUS-014 sc.4/PU-12) — warning shown, syncs to the active account, **NO proration**, effective the next unbilled period, and issued bills stay untouched (money corrections travel via J6, never via a plan edit). If the override silently prorated or reached history, an admin fixing a typo would move money.

**Fixture.** Tenant brazos-bend · premises Vega Apts, unit 104, OCCUPIED by ACC-1044 (active) · unit wrongly assigned "Small Commercial", correct plan "Residential Standard" · correction made Jul 20, current unbilled period = Jul cycle already partially elapsed; July was billed in error in June? No — June's bill is issued and wrong-plan-priced (the J6 correction fixture).

**Fixture amendment (2026-08-22, CR-014 · DEF-556).** Seeded by `seed_journey_fixture.seed_t4_03`. The occupied unit is **106** with **ACC-2046** active, and the already-issued wrong-plan-priced bill is **August's**, not June's — a June service date posts its journal entry inside the finance-signed LE-1 window (cutover Jun 30 → Jul 31). What this journey asserts is that an issued wrong-plan bill EXISTS and can only be corrected through J6; the month it was issued in is not asserted, and its total is engine-computed per 00-e2e-design.md §5.
