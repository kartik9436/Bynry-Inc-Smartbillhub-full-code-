**Intent.** The month the product becomes the **system of record for both sides** and **processes neither** (PO 2026-08-12, OQ-188). A clerk captures what the utility was invoiced — bulk water, purchased power, gas, O&M, meters — with an **optional** volume (OQ-190); the reconciliation produces the one figure no accounting package can (**bought 1,000,000 gallons, billed 910,000 → 9.00% unaccounted for**); the same view **refuses to produce it** the moment a purchase arrives without a volume, naming the reason instead of printing **−13.75%**; and the closed month hands **both sides out in ONE idempotent batch**. Every gate that guards the receivable side — closed-period-required, idempotency, drill-back, RLS — is exercised against the payable side and **none of them is loosened**.

**Fixture.** Tenant **brazos-bend** · period **July 2026** · nine captured supplier invoices **CI-1..CI-9 totalling $51,902.50 USD** (`09-ledger/08-worked-examples.md` §LE-11) · billed volumes Water **910,000 gal** · Electric **389,340 kWh** · Gas **18,900 ccf** · chart cost section 5100 · 5110 · 5120 · 5130 · 5140 · 5200 · 5300, every row **marked-unsigned pending the B5 re-sign** (BR-LED-037) · July closes **Aug 5** (LE-9) · export target QuickBooks, batch **EXP-2607-QBO** (LE-5, now carrying both sections) · actors **Norma Reyes** (Billing Clerk), **Alex Reed** (Executive, read-only), **Dana Fox** (System Admin) · isolation tenant **Lone Star Ridge** (J. Okafor, `LSR-BW-0091`, 220,000 gal / $842.00).

⚠ **Two steps of this journey are BLOCKED and are written as refusals, not as guesses.** Whether a capture POSTS a journal entry is **OQ-200** and whether an invoice may be edited after its period closes is **OQ-203**; both boundaries assert a stub, never a chosen behaviour (CLAUDE.md prime directive).
