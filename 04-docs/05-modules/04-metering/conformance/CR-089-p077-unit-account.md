## 11 · Specification table — pages 81–83, verbatim

**FOR** — *"A clerk on the phone to a resident; a supervisor about to send
someone to disconnect; anyone preparing a move-out or arguing about an opening
balance."*

**HOW YOU GET HERE** — *"The unit's page → Account. `Open the account →` leads to
everything this tab deliberately does not duplicate: the bill list, the payment
history, the correspondence."*

**PRIMARY ACTION** — *"`Open the account →`. `Move-out…` is danger tier and is
honestly held today — move-out closes the account, takes a final read and raises
the final bill, and that flow lives on the Accounts screen with no door from
here."*

**COGNITIVE LOAD** — *"One banner when there is one, three headed groups of six,
ten and two rows, two notes, two buttons. A reader looking for one answer reads
one group."*

⚠ **THAT SENTENCE IS THE FRAME'S OWN ARITHMETIC AND IT AGREES WITH IT EXACTLY:**
6 + 10 + 2 = the eighteen rows counted in §6–§8. It is the strongest evidence in
the section that the drawn row set is deliberate and complete.
