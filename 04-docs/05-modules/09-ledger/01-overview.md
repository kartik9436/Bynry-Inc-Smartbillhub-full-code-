**v5.3 update (2026-08-15 — THE PAYABLE SIDE ARRIVES, and it lives here).** The Product Owner ruled on 2026-08-12 (**OQ-188 CLOSED**, verbatim *"we can make this system of record and capture Ap, AR both ; we dont have to process anything just capture and help Utility, Sub metering copany or HOA reconcile both sides as system of record"*) that the product captures the **cost of service** — purchased power, purchased water, purchased gas, O&M, infrastructure — alongside the receivables it already holds, and **processes neither side**: no approvals, no payment runs, no vendor master, no ageing chase, no cheque printing. Full accounts payable, option (c), was **declined**. A captured invoice carries an **OPTIONAL volume** (**OQ-190 CLOSED**, *"make a provision and make it optional"*), which is what lets the product compute the one figure no accounting package can: **bought 1,000,000 gallons, billed 910,000 → 9.00% unaccounted for** — leak, meter under-registration or theft — and the figure is **all-or-nothing per service per period**, reported **ABSENT with its reason** rather than derived from a partial denominator (which reads **−13.75%** on the worked case). **NO FIFTEENTH MODULE WAS MINTED** — Eng-Lead disposition, decision log 2026-08-15: the chart, the posting service, month close and the export are all ledger-owned, so AP capture inside the ledger calls them directly instead of buying a boundary and immediately needing a hole in it (hard rule 5). **What landed:** epic **EP-LED-06**, stories **US-LED-017..021**, rules **BR-LED-028..039**, fixtures **LE-11..LE-14 / V-8..12**, journey **J25**, screens **S73/S74** (provisional). **What did NOT:** the schema migration (B2 addendum, schema route) and any edit to **B5 line 74 ❄️** — the proposed cost-of-service chart section (5100 · 5110 · 5120 · 5130 · 5140 · 5145 · 5200 · 5300) is **UNSIGNED and needs a finance re-sign via HUMAN-GUIDE §6**, the route the 4160/4170 codes and the concession account are already on. **Five questions the rulings do not settle are OPEN and nothing guesses them: OQ-200** (does a capture POST, and what is the credit side — the fork moves LE-1/GT-51 from 598.69 to 52,501.19) · **OQ-201** (the purchase-unit vocabulary per service) · **OQ-202** (which bills form the billed denominator — the basis swings the headline figure from 9.00% to 11.55%) · **OQ-203** (edit after the period closes) · **OQ-204** (ratify S73/S74 into A1;
