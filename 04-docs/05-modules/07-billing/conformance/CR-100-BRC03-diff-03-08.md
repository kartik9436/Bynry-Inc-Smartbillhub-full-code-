## ⛔ THE BRIEF'S QUESTION, ANSWERED: WHAT `BIL-S90` ACTUALLY COSTS

**`BIL-S90` is ranked FIRST of the nine that cost the most, and its stated cost is:**

| *"Not a slow run — no run"* | True of the refusal's timing and that is the design: it refuses **at the door, before any bill is drafted**, in two seam queries, naming the count, the ceiling and the minutes. It is a refusal with a remedy, not a wall. | `run_jobs.py:252–262` |
