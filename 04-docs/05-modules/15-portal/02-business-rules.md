# 15 · Customer Web Portal — business rules

**Family:** `BR-CWP-###` · **Pattern:** `BR-CWP-\d{3,5}(?!\d)` — the lookahead is
load-bearing (`DEF-2804`) · **Zero-padded to three, chosen at birth**, the same spelling
`CWP` screen ids took, because this series carries no citations yet and padding a series
that already has them is what law 11 forbids.

**Status vocabulary.** `CONFIRMED` cites the ruling or signed document it comes from ·
`PROPOSED` cites the derivation and is ours to decide and write down · `OPEN` cites the
question that blocks it and **may not be answered by a lane**.
