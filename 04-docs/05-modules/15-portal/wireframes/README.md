# Wireframes — one image per screen, cropped from the blueprint

**Source:** *SMARTBillHub Customer Portal · Blueprint, read of 2.125.4* (the PDF in the
Product Owner's Drive folder, 4.3 MB, 163 pages). **Rendered 2026-09-03 at 110 dpi with
PyMuPDF**, each image cropped from the screen's heading to the next screen's heading, and
stitched where a drawing crossed a page break. Palette-quantised to 64 colours; total
**3.9 MB** for 76 files.

**These are the layout reference `08-screen-layouts.md` was written from, and the picture
hard-won law 6 says to diff the running app against.** A builder opens the image beside the
story. Nothing here is the design authority over B14 — where a drawing and a B14 rule
disagree, B14 wins and `08-screen-layouts.md` says where.

`CWP-076-079.png` is the blueprint's phone page (`M1`–`M4`) — **phone layouts are deferred
by PO instruction 2026-09-03** (*"Dont worry about mobile yet"*) and no layout entry is
written for them.
