# customers — Flows & States

Screens: S17–S24 Accounts list/detail/wizards (details: A1 screen inventory, A9 navigation map).
State machines relevant here: see A5. Extracted with the EP-CUS pack (2026-08-08):

- **Account:** pending (draft/created) → active (create/move-in, US-CUS-003/004) → closed (move-out, US-CUS-005). Aging labels (Current/30d/60+) are DERIVED, never stored (BR-CUS-016). Extended A5 vocabulary (Pending activation, Marked disconnect, Excluded) → OQ-058.
- **Plan assignment:** append-only history on BOTH sides — `unit_plan_assignments` (metering/rating side, r5 #13) and `account_plan_assignments` (PROPOSED-SCHEMA, BR-CUS-029); sources new_connection | move_in | change_plan; billing prices from the account snapshot as of service date (BR-CUS-014 sc.6).  ⚠ **`account_plan_assignments` is SUPERSEDED (PO 2026-08-11)** — no account-side plan table is built; the plan lives once on `units.rate_plan_id` and the account derives it. See BR-CUS-029.
- **Deposit:** none → held (collect/carry/first-bill, US-CUS-007) → **settled at CLOSURE against EVERYTHING OUTSTANDING**, remainder refunded (BR-CUS-025 as amended PO 2026-08-12, OQ-158 *"everything outstanding"* — a **$120.84** arrear sits inside the settled base, so the product stops refunding **$90.14** to a customer in debt to it); waived-with-reason path; interest per BR-CUS-057 (OQ-061 CLOSED). ⚠ **THE DEPOSIT NEVER MOVES MID-TENANCY (PO 2026-08-12, OQ-179):** a plan change computes, quotes and bills **no** differential — BR-CUS-042 and BR-CUS-062's differential clauses are both SUPERSEDED, and **neither −$18.50 nor −$68.50 is ever shown to a customer**.
- **Payment plan:** proposed → agreement_sent → active (on e-signature) → closed (**final installment OR the utility writing the arrears off** — PO 2026-08-12, OQ-171: *"Write-off closes - these are user business decision... we have to provide a mechanism and report it"*; a **$267.30** plan with only its **$50.00** down payment stays live for eighteen months until someone decides); ~~PROPOSED `defaulted` on the template default clause~~ **SUPERSEDED PO 2026-08-11 (OQ-141) — no `defaulted`, no void, nobody dropped** (BR-CUS-044). Dunning + disconnect paused while active + current — and **"current" ends the day an instalment is missed, with no grace** (PO 2026-08-12, OQ-170: PE-13's **$36.22** unpaid 1 Oct → chasing resumes **2 Oct**). **An instalment payment reaches the PLAN first** (PO 2026-08-12, OQ-139 → BR-PAY-012); short of it, nothing reaches the current bill.
- **Unit occupancy (owned by metering, driven from here):** vacant → occupied on account activation; back to vacant on close — always DERIVED (BR-MET-036); unit keeps its last plan as prefill (BR-MET-044).

Flow chains: S17 → S18/S19/S20 (lifecycle); S21 → S22 modals (actions), S23 (change plan), S24 (payment plan); S20 step 4 → chained S19 (next tenant — same-day edge OQ-060). Correction flow for money on issued bills is J6, never these wizards (BR-CUS-014 sc.4).
