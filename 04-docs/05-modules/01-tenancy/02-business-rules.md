| BR-TEN-035 | Numbering: literal prefix + `{YYYY}/{YY}/{MM}/{DD}` + one zero-padded sequence token; allocation inside the issuing transaction from a per-tenant per-series monotonic counter (gaps ok, never reused); format changes apply to future numbers only — issued numbers never renumbered; next-number edits forward-only. ⚠ **EXTENDED 2026-08-17 by CR-005 (PO 2026-08-16, SET-08 Numbering).
