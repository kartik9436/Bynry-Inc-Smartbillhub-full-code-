## What this screen does NOT draw, and why each

* **No `verdict` band.** The outcome of a test bill is a bill or a stop, and the `sentence` block is the
  one whose stated job is *"a stop, a find, read this aloud"* (`09-component-inventory.md` part 1, block
  8). A verdict band would print the same outcome a second time, which is the description budget rule 32
  exists to protect.
* **No `downloads` button, and this one is a live finding rather than a preference.** The coverage table
  says every runbook except 4, 6 and 13 draws one — **and runbook 4 is the closest analogue this
  catalogue has** (one account, side-by-side lines, one outcome card) and carries none. Underneath that:
  the two result files stream `runbook_run_records`, **and nothing writes a record row yet** —
  `close_run` freezes the report and writes no records, so the button would download an empty file. A
  control over nothing is the dead-door shape hard-won law 2's 2026-09-05 mirror is about. **When a wave
  persists record rows, this screen gains a `downloads` block and this paragraph is what to delete.**
* **No `chain`.** Four steps, and three of them are invisible to a clerk who has the answer in front of
  them. The chain earns its place on runbook 1, where *which of ten steps stopped* IS the finding.
