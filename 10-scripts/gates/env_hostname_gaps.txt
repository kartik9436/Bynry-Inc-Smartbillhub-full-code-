#   EXPIRING  when the copy stops naming a host, this line is STALE and G19
#             FAILS until it is deleted — in the same diff that fixed the copy.

#

#   <catalogue key> <DEF-###> <YYYY-MM-DD parked> <reason, and what closes it>
